Measure first, and less is wasted

  • Water left alone

    A cup that reads in range skips the filter. Nothing is spent on water that didn't need it.

  • Filters used to the end

    The page says when a cartridge is spent, from its retest or its litre count, so it isn't binned early or kept too long.

  • Places nobody samples

    Hobby boards and a radio link with no router or cell, so a cup can sit where the last published sample is years old.

AI that explains, inside limits

  • Gemini chooses the filter if it answers within six seconds. If not, the tank's own rule does, so the cup always moves.
  • After each retest, a team of LangChain deep agents, a lead and five specialists, reads the base station's own checks, this site's history, nearby nodes, filter life and published EPA and AWWA guidance, and writes what to do next.
  • Neither will call water safe. Three readings can't show that.
