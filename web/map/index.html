What am I looking at?
Glass tanks
Each tank is one test site. The water level is the first pour's dissolved solids (full is 1,000 ppm) and its color is the verdict. Gray tanks are simulated. Click one for its history.
The blue line
How treated water reaches each site: the plants, the Princeton Ridge tower, then the sites. Real pipe locations aren't public, so the paths are approximate.
Blue rings and blue text
Context, not our readings: USGS stream readings nearby, the plants and the tower.
Colored dots (zoomed out)
Public samples of rivers, lakes and wells across the US since 2024: blue under 500 ppm, rose over it. That is the EPA's drinking-water guideline, shown for scale; this is untreated water.
Thin blue arcs
Radio links: each site sends its readings to the base station by radio. Hover an arc for its signal strength; switch them off in View and layers.
