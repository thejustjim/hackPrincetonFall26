What am I looking at?
Glass tanks
Each tank is one test site. The water level inside shows the TDS of the first pour: the higher the water, the more dissolved minerals and salts (full tank = 1,000 ppm). The water color is the verdict. Gray, faded tanks are simulated sites from the gateway, not real hardware. Hover a tank for its readings, click it for history.
Blue lines
How treated water gets to each site: from the two treatment plants, south to the water tower on Princeton Ridge, then out to the test sites. Real pipe locations aren't public, so the paths are approximate.
Treatment plants
Raritan-Millstone and Canal Road, run by New Jersey American Water. They take river and canal water, filter it and disinfect it.
Water towers
The light gray towers are real mapped water towers. They hold treated water up high so gravity keeps pressure steady in the pipes.
Base station and arcs
Each site radios its readings to the base station over LoRa. Arc color shows signal strength: blue is strong, gray is weak.
Stream labels
USGS readings from nearby streams, for comparison with the tap water.
Colored dots (zoomed out)
13,890 public water samples across the US since 2024, colored by TDS.
Buildings
Real Princeton buildings with mapped heights. Hover one for its name and height.
